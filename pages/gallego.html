El Gallego Todo Motor

A wholesale and retail store for an engine-parts business. With more than 30 years in the automotive market and 8 branches across Buenos Aires, El Gallego Todo Motor sells engine parts for every make and model, light and heavy line, to professionals and individuals alike.

I built a complete eCommerce on WordPress and WooCommerce that unifies retail and wholesale: more than 13,000 retail products with public prices and more than 50,000 wholesale-only products behind restricted access and preferential pricing, plus the admin dashboards to run it all.

Context

The business needed to modernise how it sells and give both retail and wholesale customers an efficient way to buy online — without running two separate stores.

A catalog for retail customers

Retail customers browse the public catalog with visible prices and filter by car brand and category; every filter shows how many products match, to narrow down a 60,000+ part catalog.

Products for retail customers — filtering by brand and category

A dedicated area for wholesalers

Approved wholesalers get their own panel: a fast order table with brand, category, sub-category, SKU and price where they can set quantities and add to cart row by row, their purchase history, and full price lists to download by brand or product.

Wholesale-only order table

Spreadsheet downloads for wholesalers, per brand

Roles, pricing and admin dashboards

Behind the store, a custom admin panel separates retail and wholesale users. Access can be granted from a dashboard or approved by email, and discounts are applied automatically per brand or per customer. Dashboards per shop and per branch track operations, stock and sales, and prices and stock are managed centrally.

Wholesale price-adjustment dashboard (customer data blurred)

The hard part: the data

  • Messy source files — several Excel sheets with inconsistent information had to be cleaned and standardised before import.
  • No images or categories — most of the 60,000+ products had neither, so I set up a loading and organisation workflow to complete the catalog visually and improve navigation.
  • Duplicates and broken fields — automatic validations plus manual cleanup to keep the final catalog consistent.
  • Moving requirements — the scope changed during development and the build had to adapt along the way.

Results

The catalog is centralised and kept up to date; retail and wholesale customers see the right prices automatically; branch dashboards and spreadsheet downloads removed manual work and loading errors; and the structure can take new brands, users and payment methods without hurting performance. Wholesalers now order more than 50% faster with direct online access and exclusive prices.
