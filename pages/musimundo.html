Musimundo

SAP-integrated scanning on the shop floor. For Grupo Carsa (operator of Musimundo), I worked onsite on the installation and supervision of SAP-integrated handheld scanning devices used for product management.

The role involved configuring scanning hardware, validating data synchronization with the server database, and ensuring reliable operation within a real-world retail environment.
