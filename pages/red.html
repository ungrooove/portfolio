RE&D Legal Group

Legal and logistics support, every step of the way. RE&D Legal Group involved the development of two tailored solutions.

The first was a WordPress-based landing page built with JavaScript to present legal professionals and institutional information. The second was a fully custom property management platform, developed using PHP, JavaScript, and Advanced Custom Fields (ACF), featuring custom admin dashboards that allow administrators to create, edit, and delete property listings through an optimized internal workflow.

The firm

The landing page introduces a young, multidisciplinary firm: the logo and a single call to action (a free case evaluation) up front, followed by the firm’s story, mission, vision and values, the management team and the practice areas — corporate, civil & commercial, family, real estate and intellectual property — plus publications, customs and a contact form.

A custom property platform

The second piece is a listings platform built from scratch rather than with a real-estate plugin. Visitors can search and open advanced filters; each card shows whether the property is for rent or sale, the price in USD, location, type, bedrooms, bathrooms, surface and amenities.

Behind it, custom admin dashboards built with ACF let the team create, edit and delete listings without going through the generic WordPress editor.
